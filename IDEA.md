# Surprise 8: DAYLIGHT (nixfred.daylight)
Sun and moon for Atlanta computed locally (NOAA algorithm, no network). Bar: sun/moon glyph + time to next sunrise/sunset. Panel: daylight arc with the sun position now, golden/blue hour, day length trend vs yesterday, moon phase + illumination, next full/new moon.
Why: Fred works through the night (sessions at 2am); knowing daylight left and the moon is delightful and daily.
Runner-ups: Weather (exists), World clocks (Chronos).
